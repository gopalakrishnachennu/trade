When live data passes the threshold, the supervisor rolls rows dated before today (ET) out of the live CSVs, rotated event files and logs into data/archive/part-NNNN-<start>-<end>.tar.zst with a manifest (sha256, row counts, time range). The sqlite ledger stays live and continuous; each part carries a consistent VACUUM INTO snapshot of it. The Mac pulls parts over HTTPS (scripts/local_sync/pull_archives.sh), verifies sha256, stores them in ~/PolymarketData/ and POSTs an ack. Only acknowledged parts are pruned (old rejected orders and equity snapshots leave the live sqlite; the tarball is deleted a few days later). Unacknowledged data is never deleted.
