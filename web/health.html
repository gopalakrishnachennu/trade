CRITICAL components latch HALT when FAILED (two consecutive checks for system probes; trading heartbeats via the watchdog). HALT stops new entries and parks the trading processes; settlement of open paper positions continues on restart. HALT is cleared only on the droplet with scripts/reset_halt.sh --yes after the cause is understood — never from this page.
NON-CRITICAL components (LLM reviewer, archive job, sync acks, TLS, dashboard, strategy lab, alerts) are shown DEGRADED/FAILED and alerted, but never halt trading.
Machine-readable: /healthz (public, status word only) · /metrics (Prometheus, token required).
